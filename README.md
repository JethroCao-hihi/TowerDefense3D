🛸 InfinityMaze - 3D Tower Defense Engine 🛡️

"Defend in 3D space, destroy the Boss, protect the base!"

📖 Overview

InfinityMaze is an exciting 3D Tower Defense game where players must face continuous waves of UFO attacks. The project focuses on real-time strategic gameplay, smart economy management, and a unique Tower Fusion (Fuse) mechanic to upgrade power.

🚀 Key Features

5-Wave Attack System: Increasing intensity from the early stages to the final destructive Boss.

Tower Fusion (Fuse) Mechanic: Combine lower-tier towers to create supreme weapons, fully restoring the new tower’s health.

UFO Counter-Attack System: Enemies are no longer passive targets — they fight back against towers, forcing players to manage tower Health Bars carefully.

Data-Driven Design: Easy game balancing through ScriptableObject files without touching the code.

Tactical Economy System: Skip Wave feature rewards players with money, balancing risk and reward.

🎮 How to Play

Build: Purchase towers from the shop and place them on empty slots.

Fuse: Drag and drop one tower onto another of the same type and level to upgrade it to a higher tier.

Manage: Monitor tower health bars. Sell damaged or poorly positioned towers to recover 50% of their value.

Win: Survive all 5 challenging waves and defeat the final UFO Boss!

🛠️ Technologies Used

Engine: Unity 6.3 LTS

Language: C#

Graphics: 3D Assets (Alienware-optimized), Unity Lighting & Post-Processing

Architecture: Singleton Pattern, Coroutine-based Wave System, ScriptableObjects
